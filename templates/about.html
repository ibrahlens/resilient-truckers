About Resilient Truckers Foundation

Resilience in Every Mile.

Our Story

Resilient Truckers Foundation is a legally registered non-profit organization committed to improving the welfare and quality of life of truck drivers across Kenya.

Every day, truck drivers work tirelessly to transport food, medicine, fuel, construction materials and countless other essential goods that keep our nation moving. Their dedication powers businesses, connects communities and supports Kenya's economic growth.

Despite their vital contribution, many truck drivers face demanding working conditions, long periods away from their families, health challenges, financial pressures and limited access to welfare and support services. These realities inspired the creation of our foundation.

We exist to provide practical assistance, promote healthier lifestyles, encourage continuous professional development and build a strong, supportive community where every truck driver feels valued, respected and empowered to thrive.

Guided by our motto, "Resilience in Every Mile," we are committed to creating lasting positive change by improving drivers' well-being, promoting road safety and strengthening the communities that depend on them every day.

What We Offer

Hospitality

Providing a welcoming and supportive environment for truck drivers.

Moral, Social & Financial Guidance

Helping drivers and their families make informed life and financial decisions.

Progressive Refresher Training

Continuous professional training to improve road safety and driving excellence.

Regular Medical Checks

Promoting healthy lifestyles through regular medical checkups and wellness programs.

Why Truck Drivers Matter

Truck drivers are the backbone of Kenya's economy. They connect farmers to markets, businesses to customers, hospitals to medical supplies and families to essential goods.

By investing in truck drivers, we strengthen supply chains, improve road safety, support families and contribute to national development.

Our Impact

Every journey we make together creates stronger drivers, safer roads and healthier communities.
