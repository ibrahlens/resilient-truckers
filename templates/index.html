Who We Are

Resilient Truckers Foundation is a Kenyan non-profit organization dedicated to improving the welfare of truck drivers and their families through education, healthcare, road safety, and community support.

Our Programs

Road Safety

Training drivers to reduce accidents and improve safety on Kenyan roads.

Healthcare

Medical camps, wellness checks and mental health support.

Financial Literacy

Helping truck drivers manage finances and plan for their future.

Why Choose Resilient Truckers Foundation?

We are committed to making a lasting difference in the lives of truck drivers and their families through practical programs, community engagement, and compassionate support.

🚛 Driver Focused

Every initiative is designed around the real challenges faced by truck drivers on Kenyan roads.

❤️ Community First

We believe healthy drivers build stronger families and safer communities.

🛡️ Safety Driven

Road safety education remains at the heart of everything we do.

🌍 National Vision

Our goal is to reach truck drivers across all 47 counties of Kenya and East Africa through sustainable programs.
